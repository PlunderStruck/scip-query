## Log

- 2026-09-02 18:20 UTC: opened. The first four rules came out of reading the
  two new repositories' samples rather than from the older label sets.
- 2026-09-02 18:35 UTC: the Stable_Management extraction labels had to be
  rewritten. They were anchored on each row's largest region, but the tier is
  decided on the most actionable region, which is often a different one. The
  tier reason now names its own line range, which is what made the mismatch
  visible.
- 2026-09-02 18:45 UTC: two candidate rules were measured and dropped rather
  than shipped. A brace-imbalance demotion fired on most rows and cost recall
  on Vega and SuplierScout; balancing the spans instead grew the finding count
  with no measured gain. What shipped is the single-statement rule, which is
  the only extraction change the labels back.
